      A private notebook made of blocks

      Write with blocks

      Headings, bulleted, numbered and to-do lists, toggles, quotes, callouts, dividers, code, tables, images and links to other pages. Type / or Markdown like # , - , [] .

      Organise and find

      Nest pages as deep as you like in the sidebar, drag to reorder, star favourites, give pages an icon and a cover, and find any word with Ctrl+K. Arabic and right-to-left text just work.

      Yours, offline, exportable

      Pages and images are saved in this browser and the app works with no connection. Export a page to Markdown, HTML or PDF, or download one backup .zip that restores everything.

      Nothing is uploaded: no account, no server, no analytics. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your pages, so keep a backup.
